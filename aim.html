Window grid

The windows sit well above the flag, because at 20 m the ball is already climbing. Set the height by eye against where her shots actually are. Widths come from the curvature maths: straight is within 1.5°, a working draw or fade runs 1.5° to 4.5°.

Calibration

Everything scales off this. To set it: stand a measured distance from two objects a measured distance apart, switch to Approach, and nudge the slider until the bracket matches them. An iPhone main camera is near 69°, the ultra wide is far wider, and any zoom changes it.

Aim scope

Shows how big the target actually is, at its true angular size, over the camera. It measures nothing and records nothing. Line it up, see what sits behind the edges on the range, and use those as the target for the session.
